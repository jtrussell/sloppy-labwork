Blue means staff. A staff element sits on the normal surface and is marked by the blue accent and the word "Staff", so it reads the same in both themes and never relies on colour alone.

  • Add .staff-only to hide an element from non-staff. This hides controls only.
  • Data and capabilities must be withheld on the server (playgroup_member.is_staff in the view or template).
  • A page only staff can open shows the staff strip under the masthead.
