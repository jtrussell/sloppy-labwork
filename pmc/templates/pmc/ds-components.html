Action row

Every group of buttons follows one rule, whichever container holds it: .card__actions in a card, .form__actions under a form, .dialog__footer in a dialog and .btn-row in the page. Below 720px buttons fill the width two per row, an odd one out takes the full row, and with three or more the solid main action gets its own row at the bottom. From 720px buttons keep their natural width; card, form and dialog actions sit on the right with the main action last. Resize the window to see both.
