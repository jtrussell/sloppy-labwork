Reference for building KeyChain pages. Every page extends the KeyChain base template and loads one stylesheet, pmc/css/keychain.css. Use the theme buttons above to preview a page in either theme; examples marked as themed show both at once.

Principles

  1. Phone first. Most players open KeyChain at an event, on a phone. Design the narrow layout first; wider screens get the same content in a centred column.
  2. Tokens only. Every colour, space, size, radius and shadow comes from tokens.css. No raw colours anywhere else.
  3. Reach for a component. Check this gallery before writing CSS. If something reusable is missing, add it to the system and to the gallery.
  4. Components carry no outer margin. Parents space their children: the page, cards and stacks add the gap.
  5. Staff is obvious. Anything only staff can see or do carries the blue staff accent and a Staff label. Hiding staff controls with CSS is fine; data and capabilities are protected on the server.
  6. Keyboard focus is visible. Never remove focus outlines.
  7. Targets are at least 44px. Buttons, menu items and nav items meet the tap size.
  8. Stay in place. Filters, toggles, tabs and pagination keep the scroll position with data-preserve-scroll.
  9. Celebrate, do not shame. Activity labels are positive; quiet playgroups get neutral facts.
