Rules

  1. Use only --space-* tokens for margin, padding and gap.
  2. Components have no outer margin. The parent spaces its children with gap (see Layout & alignment).
  3. Prefer gap between siblings; reserve margins for prose rhythm.
  4. Pick the step by relationship, not by eye: tight groups (an icon and its label) use 1–2, related items 3–4, separate blocks 5–6, sections 7–8.
