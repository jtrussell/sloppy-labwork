Page shell

page.html renders the navigation, messages, page content and footer inside main.container.stack. The container is capped at --container-max ({{ tokens_by_group.container.0.value }}) and takes 85–80% of narrower viewports. From 550px up the page is framed by the surface color on either side.

Breakpoints

Custom properties can't be used inside media queries, so breakpoints are a fixed set. Write mobile-first min-width queries.

Cluster

A wrapping row for groups of buttons, badges or metadata. Defaults to --space-4; .cluster--xs and .cluster--lg tighten or loosen it, .cluster--between and .cluster--end distribute items.

Readable width

.readable caps a block at 65 characters per line, for long prose and forms.

Alignment

  1. Buttons and text inputs share --control-height, so a button placed beside an input lines up without adjustment.
  2. Interactive targets on touch layouts are at least --tap-target square.
  3. Align to the content edge of the container; avoid one-off horizontal offsets.
  4. Within a row, align items to the center unless they're text of different sizes, which align to the baseline.
