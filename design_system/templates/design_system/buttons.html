Guidelines

  • Label buttons with a verb: "Save lineup", not "OK".
  • Use <a class="button"> for navigation and <button> for actions.
  • Group buttons with .cluster or .form-actions; never rely on button margins for spacing.
  • Every button shows a focus ring on keyboard focus — don't remove it.
