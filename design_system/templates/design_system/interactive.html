Action menu

A kebab button that reveals a short list of actions. Reach for it when a row or page header has three or more actions, or when actions share a line with a title that can grow. Keep one obvious primary action visible outside the menu when there is one.

It's a native <details>, so it opens without script. js/action-menu.js (loaded by page.html) closes it on an outside press, after an item is chosen, and on Escape; inside a dialog, Escape closes the menu before the dialog. Give the trigger an aria-label naming what the actions apply to. The trigger is .action-menu__trigger: borderless until hovered, focused or open. Items are links or submit buttons with .action-menu__item; add --danger for destructive ones and separate them with .action-menu__divider. The list opens below and right-aligned; use .action-menu--start to left-align and .action-menu--up to open upward (e.g. in a dialog footer). It can't escape an overflow container such as .table-scroll.
